# emilkowal.ski

Measured 2026-10-29 at 1440 and 390. Personal site of a design engineer. Job: say who he is and
list the work, nothing more.

## Why it works

- **One size.** 16px carries 93% of the text and 14px the rest. The name is 16px at weight 500.
  The headline-to-body ratio is 1. All hierarchy comes from ink (`#21201C` against `#63635E` on
  `#F9F9F8`), weight (400 → 500) and space.
- **Space does the sectioning.** Gaps step 16 → 64 → 128px. Section labels ("Today",
  "Projects") are just 16px medium words with a large gap above.
- **A narrow column on a wide screen.** About 640px of text column, left-aligned inside a centred
  frame. It reads like a note, not a page.
- **The work is elsewhere.** Sonner, Vaul and the courses are the portfolio; the site only
  points at them. The craft he is known for (motion) is in the products, not on the page.

## On the phone

Almost identical: same 16px, same column, same gaps. A single-column text site needs no mobile
redesign, which is its own lesson.

## Borrow

Hierarchy without size. Off-white ground with a warm near-black ink. Big vertical gaps as the
only section marker.

## Leave

The emptiness, unless the work is famous enough to carry it. The owner's blind test (B4) found a
page this austere reads as thin for a less-known developer. The probe's "no decisive size jump"
warning fires here, and here it is wrong: a ratio of 1 is the design.
