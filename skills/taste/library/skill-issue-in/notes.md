# skill-issue.in

Measured 2026-10-29 at 1440 and 390. A ranked directory of agent skills. Job: browse a long list
quickly, and remember the site.

## Why it works

- **One signature image carries the identity.** The hero is a black-and-white halftone collage
  (clouds, a torn-paper hole, an eye peering through). Below it the page is a plain list of
  bordered cards with pill tags. The cards and pills are generic; the picture is not, and it is
  enough to make the whole site memorable.
- **A logotype with a voice.** "Skill Issue" and the hero line are Instrument Serif italic, in
  mustard (`#F5D87A`) on the black art. Everything else is Geist Sans. The serif appears in two
  places only, which is why it lands.
- **Warm paper around white.** The page ground is a warm off-white (`#F4F2EE`, `#F0EEE9`); the
  content sheet is white with a 14px radius. Inks are `#151515`, `#4A4A4A` and `#6B6B6B`.
- **Pixel icons as a system.** Each skill has a small pixel-grid glyph, coloured by category
  (amber for design, blue for frontend). It is a cheap, consistent icon language that no icon
  library supplies.

## On the phone

The hero image shrinks into a framed card and the list becomes stacked cards with the actions
(open, copy) on the right edge. Structure is entirely conventional.

## Borrow

The proof that a generic structure is fine when there is one strong, owned image and a logotype
with character. A small, rule-based icon language. Warm paper as the outer ground.

## Leave

The 10–11.5px meta text (about 80% of all text by count) and the pill-on-pill tag rows. They
fail the comfort floor and are the parts that look like every directory.
