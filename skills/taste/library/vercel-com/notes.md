# vercel.com

Measured 2026-10-29 at 1440 and 390. Product site for a developer platform. Job: state the
position in one line and route three audiences (deploy, demo, sales). Some images did not load
during capture; the type and colour numbers are computed styles and are complete.

## Why it works

- **Big type at a regular weight, tracked hard.** The headline is Geist 64px at weight **400**,
  line-height 1.0, tracking **−0.06em**. Size and tight tracking give the authority; it never
  needs bold. Body and UI are 14px (63% of text), with a 450 weight in between 400 and 500.
- **Greyscale with one mark.** Ground `#FAFAFA` and white, ink `#171717`, greys `#4D4D4D`,
  `#666666`, `#8F8F8F`. The black triangle is the only image in the first screen and it is the
  logo itself, lit with a soft glow.
- **A three-part hero.** Headline and buttons on the left, the mark in the centre, three short
  lines on the right. It uses the full 1440 width without a single card.
- **Restrained buttons.** One filled black button, one outlined; the outlined ones in the nav
  are small and square-ish (6px radius), the hero ones are full pills.

## On the phone

The mark goes first, then a centred 48px headline, a mono line ("For coding agents"), and two
full-width stacked buttons. Navigation collapses to a hamburger. The three-part hero becomes one
centred column, which is the standard mobile move, done with care.

## Borrow

Display at weight 400 with negative tracking around −0.04 to −0.06em. One graphic in the hero,
and let it be the brand mark. Full-width stacked buttons on the phone. A 400/450/500 weight trio
instead of bold.

## Leave

Tracking as tight as −0.06em on anything below about 40px; it closes up. The 14px body on
phones.
