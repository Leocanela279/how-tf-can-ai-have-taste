# elevenlabs.io

Measured 2026-10-29 at 1440 and 390. Product site for an AI voice company. Job: let people hear
the product. The custom fonts and customer logos did not load during capture; the type numbers
are computed styles and are complete.

## Why it works

- **A light display face at weight 300.** Headlines are Waldenburg at weight 300, 48px at 1440
  (36px at 390), line-height about 1.1. A thin, wide display feels calm and expensive, and the
  rest of the page (Inter 400 at 15–16px) stays out of its way.
- **One image language: the voice orbs.** Each voice is a soft, blurred gradient sphere. They are
  the only colour on the page and the only imagery in the hero, and you press them to play. The
  picture *is* the product demo.
- **Warm neutrals with true black.** Grounds `#FDFCFC`, `#F5F3F1` and white; ink is pure `#000`
  for headings and warm greys `#44403B`, `#777169`, `#A59F97` below it. Black on warm paper reads
  softer than black on white.
- **Pills for action, small radii for structure.** Buttons and tabs are full pills (9999px);
  panels use 4px and 16px. Two radius families, each with one job.
- **A product picker in the hero.** Tabs (Creative / Agents / API) switch the demo underneath, so
  the first screen answers "which product is for me" by letting you try each.

## On the phone

Headline and copy first, then the tabbed orb carousel, cropped at the edges so the next orb
peeks in. The page runs about 18 screens at 390, long but always in one column.

## Borrow

A light-weight display face as the voice. Letting the product's own output be the only imagery.
Warm off-white with warm greys. One radius family per job.

## Leave

Weight 300 below about 32px, and the `#A59F97` grey for text (too light on a light ground). The
length: 12 screens at 1440 is a large company's page, not a template for small sites.
