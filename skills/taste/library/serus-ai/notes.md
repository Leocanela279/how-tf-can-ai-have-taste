# serus-ai

Measured 2026-10-29 at 1440 and 390. Site for a private AI assistant. Job: let you try the
assistant immediately, then explain the protection product. A cookie banner and a waitlist
pop-up covered part of the capture.

## Why it works

- **The hero is the product's own input.** A greeting ("Hello, I'm Serus") and a large chat box
  with a typewriter placeholder. The first action on the site is the product's first action.
- **A serif greeting over a sans system.** The greeting is Reckless (a sharp, high-contrast
  serif) at weight 400, about 40px. Everything else is Geist at 400/450/500. The serif appears
  once, as the product's voice.
- **Editorial still-life photography.** The protection product is shown as a real phone lying on
  a lavender tray with keys and sunglasses: everyday objects, shot from above, tinted to the brand
  colour. It makes "privacy" concrete without a single shield icon.
- **Lavender as the only colour.** The dotted logo mark, the sign-up button and the photo set all
  share one soft violet. Inks are near-black `#0D0D0D` at 100%, 70% and 45% alpha on `#FAFAFA`.
- **A dotted mark that animates as the thinking indicator.** The logo is a ring of dots; the same
  ring appears as the assistant's loading state. Brand and interface are one object.

## On the phone

The same centred greeting and chat box at full width, the serif at about 30px, navigation behind
a hamburger with the sign-up pill kept visible. Very little changes because the hero is a single
input.

## Borrow

Putting the product's first interaction in the hero. A one-time serif as the voice of the
product. Still-life photography of the product among real objects, tinted to one colour. A logo
that doubles as an interface state.

## Leave

13px body text (about 60% of text) and the 45%-alpha grey for anything readable. Two overlays on
first load (cookies plus a waitlist pop-up) bury the hero; on a phone the banner takes half the
screen.
