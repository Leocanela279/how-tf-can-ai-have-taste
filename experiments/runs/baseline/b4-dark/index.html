01 — Sep 2026 – now · Humannova

ICEX Next

Application portal for the ICEX Next programme. Built on the Reskillia codebase and cut down to what the client needed: no AI, no public sign-up, privacy first.

The team invites each person, who sets a password via a link, accepts the privacy policy and submits a CV and a questionnaire.

Next.js 16 · React 19 · Tailwind CSS 4 · Supabase · Docker

Visit site ↗
  • aReads CVs as PDF or images, with local OCR for scanned documents.
  • bOne questionnaire per profile, versioned, with drafts migrated between versions.
  • cEvery privacy acceptance recorded with its version and date.
  • dAnswers exported to Excel.

02 — Mar 2026 – now · Humannova

Reskillia

A platform for companies to assess their team's digital skills against DigComp 2.2.

Each person uploads a CV and fills a profile. Gemini picks seven competences with a target level, writes the questions and scores the answers. The result is a radar chart of expected vs. achieved, downloadable as PDF.

Next.js 16 · React 19 · Tailwind 4 · Supabase · Gemini · Docker

Visit site ↗
  • aEmail or Google sign-in on Supabase Auth.
  • bPersonal data encrypted with AES-256-GCM, plus RLS policies in PostgreSQL.
  • cSpanish and Catalan interface.
  • dRuns in Docker.

About

I'm a full-stack developer in Barcelona with the weight on the backend, and a computer science student. I work at Humannova. Before that, Laravel was my main tool.
